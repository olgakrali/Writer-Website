My intercosmic journey

An islander with a multidisciplinary background in Biology with specialization in Plant Biology, who moved to Sweden to explore its boreal forests. I fell in love with data analysis along the way and decided to pursue a career in computational biology within Biomedical Sciences. In 2024, after reading the book Mindset, I decided to challenge myself into something I've never dared to try before; write my own book. Creative writing became my sanctuary, helping to ease my mind during the final years of my PhD studies. My top bookself is filled with manuscripts of my stories. While the first story was inside my head for years, as the pen touched the paper the storyline changed, making me realize that I am a discovery writer. If you ask me the end of my ongoing projects, I'll tell you that I don't know.
